# Tool: Opportunity Radar

**What it does:** Surfaces a few genuinely high-fit opportunities a week — jobs, clients, collaborations, speaking, funding, whatever matters to your goals — and, just as importantly, filters out the noise. Its job is not to find *many* opportunities. It's to find the *few worth your attention*.

## Setup (ask the user)
1. What kind of opportunities are you actually looking for right now? (e.g. a senior role, new clients, speaking slots, partnerships)
2. What would make one a clear "yes"? What's an automatic "no"?
3. How many can you realistically act on in a week? (usually 1–2)

## How to run a scan
Review what's relevant (from connectors, the web, or what the user shares), then score each opportunity on four things only:
- **Fit** — does it match their goals and current focus?
- **Upside** — could it materially change their career, money, network, or visibility?
- **Effort** — can they act on it without derailing the week?
- **Deadline** — is there a real reason to act now?

Then classify each: **Act Now**, **Park**, or **Ignore**.

## Output
- **Act Now** (max 3): opportunity, why it fits, and a 20-minute first step for each.
- **Best bet:** pick ONE to prioritize, and say why.
- **Park:** worth it later, but not this week.
- **Ignored:** one line on what you filtered out and why.

## Guardrails
- Respect the capacity in `core/hot.md` — never pile on more than they can act on.
- Don't surface something just because it's interesting. It has to move a goal.
- Always include a direct link where one exists.
