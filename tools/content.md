# Tool: Content Helper

**What it does:** Turns your real work, ideas, and experiences into posts that sound like *you* — not generic AI content.

## Setup (ask the user)
1. Which platforms do you post on? (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, a newsletter…)
2. What do you want to be known for? (2–3 topics)
3. Point me to a few things you've already written or posted that sound like you — or describe your voice in a sentence.

This tool owns the `workspace/content/` folder — create it when switched on. Save the answers as `workspace/content/profile.md` so drafts stay consistent, and put every draft you write in `workspace/content/` too. Pull voice from `core/identity.md` as well.

## How to draft
Every draft should:
- Be grounded in the user's real experience or opinion — never generic aggregation.
- Sound like them (use the voice from `core/identity.md` and the content profile).
- Lead with a hook that earns attention.
- End with a human call to action, not a robotic "follow for more".

Offer a couple of angle options before writing a full draft, so the user steers direction early.

## Keep it credible
Don't invent facts, results, or stories the user didn't share. If a claim needs a real number or example, ask for it rather than fabricating.
