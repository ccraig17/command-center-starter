# Workspace

This is where your actual work lands — drafts, notes, ideas, anything the assistant produces for you day to day. Think of it as your desk.

Your assistant will organize this into subfolders that match what you make (for example `drafts/`, `notes/`, `ideas/`). It's set up during the interview based on what you told it you'll be working on. You can rename or reorganize anything in here freely — it's yours.

Bigger, longer-running initiatives live in `projects/` instead, not here.
